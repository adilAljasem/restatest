Petty Cash Management

إدارة العهدة النقدية

Requests, disbursement, settlement, replenishment and reconciliation — built for Odoo 19.

Full custody cycle

Request → approval → disbursement → settlement, with return of the remaining balance, over-spend handling and replenishment (استعاضة) that always posts a new entry instead of editing a posted one.

Finance Review

A single approval stage on settlement, granted to the standard accounting group that may create journal entries. No project, department or purchase manager approvals.

Analytic where it belongs

Each settlement line carries its own analytic distribution, applied only to that line's expense journal item. Cash, petty cash and custody lines stay clean.

What the module covers
●  Permanent and temporary petty cash journals, one of each per company
●  Settlement against vendor bills, expenses, or a plain expense account
●  Purchase taxes on settlement lines, tax-inclusive or exclusive
●  Automatic reconciliation of funding, settlement and replenishment entries
●  Excel import of settlement lines, with a sample template
●  Attachments on the request and on every settlement line
●  Record rules: a user sees their own requests and their subordinates'
●  Multi-company aware, with company checks on journals and accounts
